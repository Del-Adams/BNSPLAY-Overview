How each number is worked out

Total deposit
Deposit amount + manual deposit.
Total withdrawal
Withdrawal amount + manual withdrawal.
Net deposit
Total deposit − total withdrawal.
Conversion
New depositors ÷ registrations. Change in percentage points (pp).
GGR
Company win/loss. Positive means the house won.
Margin
GGR ÷ valid bet. Change in percentage points (pp).
All bonus ratio
Promotion ÷ GGR.
Players over several days
People repeat from day to day, so for a date range these show the average per day.
MTD
Month to date: from the 1st up to the last day of the date filter.
Active account
Host-tier agent account with P&L not zero.
Team size
TOTAL COUNTS of the agent account in the ACTIVE AGENTS sheet.
